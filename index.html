“Raises” is not the same as “harmful”: social support ▲ raises viral suppression. Entities such as age and cd4 count carry no direction of their own; open the evidence to read which end of the scale the abstract meant.

Multi-step pathways to virologic failure

A pathway is a chain of steps ending at virologic failure in which every step is supported by at least the chosen number of articles. Relation types between a pair are pooled, no other outcome may sit in between, and no entity repeats. Pathways are ranked by their weakest step (the step with the fewest supporting articles), then by total support. Click a step’s arrow to read its evidence.

    How the graph was built

    1. PubMed keyword search: 976 abstracts

    "HIV" AND "virologic failure" AND (adherence OR depression OR anxiety OR stigma OR "self-efficacy" OR "social support" OR housing OR "healthcare access" OR "substance use" OR "CD4 count" OR "viral load") AND hasabstract[text] AND ("2010"[PDAT] : "2026"[PDAT])

    2. MedCPT semantic retrieval: 387 additional abstracts

    MedCPT dense retrieval (NCBI’s contrastively pre-trained PubMed retriever), run through the LitSense 2.0 API. Five queries, written the way abstracts report findings, top 200 passages each, deduplicated by PMID:

    1. Several factors were independently associated with virologic or virological failure among people with HIV on antiretroviral therapy
    2. Treatment-related and health care factors were associated with adherence to antiretroviral therapy among people living with HIV
    3. Several factors were associated with poor adherence or nonadherence to antiretroviral therapy among people living with HIV
    4. Social and structural factors were associated with lack of viral suppression among people living with HIV
    5. Psychological, behavioral, and mental health factors were associated with viral load among people living with HIV

    MedCPT returned 431 articles. Of these, 32 were already in the keyword corpus, 10 had no abstract and 2 did not concern HIV, which leaves 387 added abstracts.

    3. Extraction

    • claude-sonnet-5 with structured outputs, 25 abstracts per request. Only relationships explicitly reported in the abstract were extracted, and null findings were excluded.
    • Seven entity categories from the Social Ecological Model: Demographic, Psychosocial, SocialEnvironmental, Treatment, AdherenceBehavior, BiologicalMarker, Outcome.
    • Every relationship carries a verbatim evidence sentence. 28 of 2,169 relationships were removed because their sentence was not found word for word in the abstract.

    4. Direction and evidence-type review

    Each relationship records two separate things:

    • Direction, as the relation name.
      • positively_associated_with: more of the subject goes with more of the object.
      • negatively_associated_with: more of the subject goes with less of the object.
      • associated_with: the sentence states no direction.
      Direction is relative to the subject and object exactly as named. It says nothing about whether an outcome is good or bad. For example, cannabis use negatively_associated_with medication adherence means that cannabis users adhere less.
    • Evidence type.
      • independent predictor: the result of a multivariable or adjusted analysis.
      • mediation: an explicit mediation analysis.
      • association: any other reported relationship.

    A language model assigned both by reading each evidence sentence. It used only what the sentence states, never background knowledge. For each relationship group, up to 5 sentences were reviewed. In the few groups with more than 5 sentences, the remaining ones take the majority result.

    4. Entity merging

    • String cleanup: lowercase, strip articles and punctuation, standard spellings (HIV-1 → hiv, antiretroviral therapy → art, virological → virologic).
    • 43 hand-written synonym rules, e.g. poor / suboptimal adherence → medication nonadherence; CD4 < 200, nadir CD4 → low cd4 count; internalized / perceived stigma → stigma.
    • Embedding clustering (all-mpnet-base-v2, cosine ≥ 0.88). A merge is blocked when the phrases differ in polarity (high vs low), a category word (male vs female, NNRTI vs INSTI) or a number, or when neither phrase’s content words contain the other’s.
    • Category by majority vote; identical subject–relation–object triples pooled with their PMIDs.

    Limitations

    • Some entities (age, cd4 count) carry no direction; read the evidence sentence.
    • Article counts reflect how often a finding is reported, not effect size or study quality.
    • A manual check of 40 random relationships in the keyword-only pilot found 90% supported by their evidence; the merged graph has not been re-reviewed.
